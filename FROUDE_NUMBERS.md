# Froude Numbers at Gait Transitions (Various Species)

- **Humans (Homo sapiens):** Walk→run transition at Fr ≈0.50【14†L312-L319】.  (Measured on 1 g treadmill in adults of varying leg length; consistent under reduced gravity.)  
- **Domestic cat (Felis catus):** Walk→trot at Fr ≈0.50【44†L293-L295】.  (Healthy adult cats on a level treadmill, transition near 1 m/s.)  
- **Domestic dog (Canis familiaris) – walk→trot:** Transition at Fr ≲0.50【63†L2519-L2527】.  (Large breeds >20 kg, steady locomotion on level ground/treadmill; onset of trot around Fr 0.4–0.5.)  
- **Domestic dog (Canis familiaris) – trot→gallop:** Transition at Fr ≈0.93 (mean 0.93±0.07)【63†L2519-L2527】.  (Same study: breeds on treadmill; galloping began at Fr ~1.)  
- **Horse (Equus caballus) – walk→trot:** Transition at Fr ≈0.35【66†L314-L321】.  (Nine adult horses (90–720 kg), treadmill measurements; all switched to trot near Fr =0.35.)  
- **Horse (Equus caballus) – trot→gallop:** Transition at Fr ≈2.5【39†L203-L204】 (typical mammalian value).  (Many equids and other quadrupeds gallop when Fr ≈2.5; e.g. gait-change theory from dynamic similarity.)  
- **Ostrich (Struthio camelus):** Walk→run transition at Fr ≈0.20【86†L369-L374】.  (Treadmill study: 2.1 m/s transition speed for adults, hip height ~1.10 m ⇒ Fr≈0.20.)  
- **Emu (Dromaius novaehollandiae):** Walk→run transition at Fr ≈0.18【86†L375-L379】.  (Treadmill: ~1.9 m/s transition, hip height ~0.85 m ⇒ Fr≈0.18; birds alternated gaits around this.)  
- **Elephant (Elephas maximus; Loxodonta africana):** No true walk→run; maintained a slow “amble” up to Fr ≳3.0【99†L97-L100】.  (Video of free-moving Asian elephants: even at top speeds Fr up to 3.4, gait remained a compliant walk with no aerial phase.)  
- **Quadrupedal mammals (general):** Walk→trot at Fr ≈0.50【39†L203-L204】.  (Alexander [39]: across diverse mammals (rodents→giraffes), gait change from walking to trotting typically near Fr 0.5.)  
- **Quadrupedal mammals (general):** Trot→gallop at Fr ≈2.5【39†L203-L204】.  (Alexander [39]: varied mammals generally transition from trot to gallop near Fr 2.5.)  
- **Cheetah (Acinonyx jubatus):** Trot→gallop at Fr ≈2.5【39†L203-L204】.  (Large felid carnivores likely follow similar dynamics; predicted by similarity arguments.)  
- **Sprawling lizards (e.g. green iguana *Iguana iguana*):** Walk→run around Fr ≈1【81†L1668-L1670】.  (Theoretical pendulum limit: lizards can “walk” up to Fr ~1.0 before gravity precludes inverted-pendulum gait【81†L1668-L1670】.)  

**Notes:** Measurements varied by method (treadmill vs overground), substrate (level ground, pen, etc.), and animal size/age. All values come from empirical studies. The broad range of Fr at transitions—0.18 (emu) to >3 (elephants)—shows that gait-change Froude numbers are *not* constant across taxa.
